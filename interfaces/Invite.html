Interface Invite

A pending invitation to a space (pull-based sharing, FILE_SHARING_SPEC §6.4). It grants NO access until accepted — the recipient accepts it from their inbox (listMyInvites → acceptInvite), materializing membership. The display fields (name/login/avatarUrl) are untrusted for rendering.
